How pair stock is worked out

  • A set is a top plus a bottom. Vinculum holds the kurta and the pant as separate pieces, so a set can only sell as many times as its scarcer piece allows.
  • Pairs on hand: the lower of total tops and total bottoms across both warehouses and all open stores. Dupattas are optional and not counted.
  • Warehouse pairs: the same sum using only Noida and Chhatarpur physical stock.
  • In production: pieces sitting in the virtual production bin. Not on the shelf yet.
  • Left out: VIR-STORE, the operations location, closed stores and in-transit stock.
  • A star after the number means no Vinculum match was found and Shopify's figure is shown.

What each verdict means

  • Push: proven demand, at least 21 days of cover in real pairs, page converts.
  • Test: launched in the last 30 days with 30+ pairs and images ready.
  • Hold: under 21 days of cover, stock still in production, core sizes broken, high reversals, or steady without being a hero.
  • Stop: was selling, now under 8 pairs or under 10 days of cover.
  • Fix page: 2,000+ landing sessions and a buy rate far below the store median.
  • Clear: 80+ pairs, 2 or fewer sold in 30 days, older than 120 days.

How the score is built

  • Demand 30%: units sold in 30 days, log scale, 60 units is full marks.
  • Stock 25%: the lower of pairs on hand (60 is full marks) and days of cover (45 is full marks).
  • Page conversion 15%: add-to-cart and purchase rate of landing sessions, store median scores 50. Needs 300+ sessions.
  • Momentum 10%, freshness 10%, content 10%: 7-day run rate, days live, images and description.
  • Reversals above 30% take 8 points off. A new-customer share of 60% or more adds 4.

Read these before acting

  • Pieces are matched by name and colour, not by SKU. Open Detail to see which Vinculum pieces a set was paired from.
  • Days of cover uses online sales only against stock that stores also sell from, so real cover is shorter.
  • Single ad means its own ad was active on 21 Nov. Via catalogue means 50 or more sessions from Meta ads landed on its product page between 14 and 20 Nov, read from Shopify campaign tags. In collection ad means it sits in a collection page that ads send people to: Festive Edit, Makhmal Velvet, Bags, Footwear or Mulmul Oon. None seen means none of these, which is not proof it appears in no ad.
  • Ad traffic and ad orders count sessions that landed directly on the product page. About 70% of Meta ad sessions land on collection pages, mostly Festive Edit, and cannot be tied to one product.
  • Ad spend covers single-product ads only, matched by ad name, for the last 7 days (14 to 20 Nov). Catalogue, collection and video-mix ads cannot be tied to one product. ROAS is Meta’s website purchase ROAS and is shown for reference, not scored. Spend in red means ads are running on a product with almost no pairs.
  • Sessions are landing sessions only, mostly ad traffic.
  • Free potli bags, gift cards, gift box, perfume, dupattas and separate pieces are left out.
